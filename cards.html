Questions

Are these real cards?

Yes. Each card is a professionally graded card stored in Courtyard's insured vault and represented by an NFT on Polygon. Whoever holds the NFT can trade it or redeem it for the physical card.

Which card does the vault buy?

The cheapest graded card listed on OpenSea in the coin's category (Pokémon or One Piece) at the time the vault can afford it. The keeper checks each listing's name and traits, so a basketball card never ends up in a Pokémon vault.

How do I get a card I win?

The card NFT is sent on Polygon to the same wallet address that held the coin. You can see it on OpenSea or Courtyard, keep it, sell it, or redeem it on Courtyard to have the physical card shipped.

How does ETH get from the vault to Polygon?

The keeper announces each withdrawal one hour ahead, and the Registry owner can cancel it. It then bridges exactly that ETH to Polygon (as USDC or POL, whichever the card is listed in), buys the card, and records the purchase and its Polygon transaction on Robinhood Chain.

Can I keep the cards in the vault instead?

Yes. At launch you choose what happens to each card: raffle it to holders, keep it in the vault, or burn it. The choice is permanent.
